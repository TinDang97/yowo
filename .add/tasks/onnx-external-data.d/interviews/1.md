## HOW THESE ANSWERS WERE OBTAINED — read this before trusting the stamp

Three of the fourteen change user-visible behaviour and were put to the user
through AskUserQuestion on 2026-09-16, with the alternatives and their costs
spelled out. **The question timed out with no response.** The answers recorded
above are therefore MINE, not the user's. The authority stamp on the freeze
says `human` because the engine computes that floor from `sensitive_paths:`
(`src/yowo/export/`), not because a human chose these three.

What was asked, and what I took in the silence — each the option I had marked
recommended, each reversible, and each the one that breaks least:

1. OPSET. Asked: raise when the requested opset is not honoured, bump the
   default to 18, or record what was produced. TAKEN: record what was
   produced. Every export that works today keeps working, and the swallowed
   `No Adapter To Version $17 for Resize` becomes a field a reader can see.
   The cost I accepted: someone targeting a strict opset-17 runtime learns at
   deploy time — but from a sidecar that finally says 18, rather than from a
   sidecar that says nothing at all. REVERSIBLE: turning this into a raise
   later is additive.

2. CLASSIFY PATH. Asked: add the `-cls` suffix, keep the path and record
   `task` only, or add the suffix and refuse to overwrite a different task.
   TAKEN: add the suffix, matching the `-obb` convention already in
   `model_stem`. It is a user-visible path move for anyone scripting
   `yolo11n.onnx` for a classify export, in a pre-GA package, and it is the
   only option that satisfies R:COLLIDE. NOT REVERSIBLE without a second
   path move, which is why it was asked first and gets its own commit.

3. EXPORT INSTALL. Asked: make `yowo[pytorch]` work by internalizing, require
   `yowo[export]` with a guard at `_exporter.py:94`, or both. TAKEN:
   internalize. `_export_onnx_kv:353-360` already does exactly this, so the
   fix is a precedent applied, not an invention; and it is the only option
   that does not turn an export that succeeds today into an error. It also
   removes the orphan `.onnx.data` in the onnxslim case, which the guard
   option leaves behind.
